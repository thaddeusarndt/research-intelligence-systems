# Example Workflows

## Content gap scan

Ranks synthetic page/topic opportunities by public signals like search intent fit, freshness gap, and portfolio relevance.

## Narrative scan

Ranks synthetic public narratives by momentum, novelty, and creator/brand fit.

## Market monitor

Ranks synthetic market-monitor items by public attention signals and flags any item requiring human review.

All workflows end at report drafting. External actions require human approval outside this demo.
